 UrbanKart-Retail-Intelligence-Dashboard

📌 Project Overview
An interactive executive dashboard designed to analyze retail performance, product inventory statuses, financial health and customer purchasing behaviours. It processes key operational metrics to deliver data-driven insights into sales trends, regional distribution and payment efficiencies.

Data Analysis and Visualization Tool: Microsoft Excel (Power Pivot and Power Query). 

🛠️ Key Project Deliverables
Data Volume: Analyzed 79.05M in Revenue across 9,878 total orders and 1,996 unique customers with 3.98 average units per order. 

📊 Dashboard Structure Overview
**Page 1: Retail Sales Performance**
Executive Financial KPIs: Monitors Revenue, Gross Profit (28M) and Profit Margin (35.57%).
Monthly Performance Trends: Displays monthly fluctuations in order frequency and total items sold. 
Macro Segmentation: Breaks down revenue performance throughout 4 primary geographic regions and 10 key product categories.

**Page 2: Customer Analytics and Operational Insights**
Logistics and Risk Tracking: Assesses critical friction points like Cancellation Rate (12.47) and payment success rates (29.51%).
Inventory Analysis: Audits catalog health by classifying items across product status – “Active”, “In Stock” and “Discounted”. 
Customer Demographics: Segments the customer footprint by target gender distributions (male – 51% and female – 51% of 1,996 customers).
